As we have already seen in the Introductory of Transfer Learning now we step on to an interesting part where we are actually gonna change the learning rates just in the middle when the program is actually running 

This ALR ensures that it addresses most of the problems arose by ==irregular learning rates,== 
In short - Keeping a higher learning rate converges the function faster but in the process of converging faster it might miss the sweet-spot(minima). essentially leading to a catastrophic failure of vanishing and exploding gradients.
